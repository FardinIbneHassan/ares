Home/Roadmap

From a question to a rover team.

The roadmap from our first idea to national and international rover challenges. We'll update it as each phase is finished.

  1. Phase 01 Done

    The spark

    We noticed that Dhaka University had no rover team and had never entered a rover challenge, so we set out to change that. We started with the system every rover depends on: power.

  2. Phase 02 Done

    Learning from real missions

    We studied how power problems hurt Spirit, Opportunity, InSight and Zhurong. From what we learned, we defined the six ARES subsystems.

    ResearchRequirements
  3. Phase 03 Ongoing

    Simulation & 3D design

    Software simulations of MPPT, load priority and survival logic, alongside 3D-designed parts for the panel mount and tilt mechanism.

    SimulationCAD3D printing
  4. Phase 04 Now

    Hardware prototype

    Building the physical energy module: the MPPT converter, piezo dust clearing, tilt assist and the power manager.

    ElectronicsFirmware
  5. Phase 05 Next

    Testing & results

    Measure tracking efficiency, dust clearing and survival behaviour, then publish the results and the live telemetry dashboard here.

  6. Phase 06 Goal

    Rover team & competitions

    Grow Solaris Tech into a full university rover team and enter national and international rover challenges, with ARES as its power system.
