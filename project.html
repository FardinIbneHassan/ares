Every watt counts on Mars.

ARES is an energy module for Mars rovers. It handles power generation, distribution and management, built around problems that real missions faced.

Stage: Prototyping Simulation Hardware 3D design & printing
01 / About

A power system that thinks for itself.

ARES stands for Autonomous Rover Energy System. It is the part of a rover that collects energy, stores it and decides where it goes.

Every feature we build answers a question a real mission had to face: what happens when dust covers the panels, when a storm hides the sun, or when the rover is parked at the wrong angle?

02 / The problem

What went wrong on real missions.

Solar-powered machines on Mars have been ended by power problems more often than by broken parts.

Mission Spirit roverYear 2009–2010

Stuck at the wrong angle

Trapped in soft sand, Spirit could not tilt its panels toward the low winter sun. Its power dropped too far to survive the cold, and it fell silent.

→ ARES: Tilt assist

Mission Opportunity roverYear 2018

Lost in a global dust storm

A dust storm covering the whole planet blocked out the sun for weeks. With its batteries drained, the rover never answered again.

→ ARES: Tiered survival mode

Mission InSight landerYear 2022

Buried under dust

InSight wasn't a rover, but its lesson applies: dust slowly built up on its panels until there wasn't enough power to keep operating.

→ ARES: Dust mitigation

Mission Zhurong roverYear 2022

Hibernation without waking

Zhurong went into hibernation for the Martian winter and did not wake up as planned. Dust build-up is the most likely cause.

→ ARES: Survival mode + dust mitigation

03 / The solution

Generate. Distribute. Manage.

ARES splits the job into three layers. Each layer has its own subsystems.

LAYER A

Generate

Pull the most power from every panel: track the maximum power point, keep the surface clean and point it at the sun.

MPPT · Dust · Tilt → LAYER B

Distribute

Send power where it matters most, based on what each subsystem needs and how important it is right now.

Load prioritization → LAYER C

Manage

Watch every rail in real time. When energy runs short, step down through survival tiers and keep the core signal alive.

Survival · Telemetry →
04 / How it works

Follow the energy.

From sunlight to subsystems, every step is measured and managed.

ARES // power flowSchematic

← Swipe to see the full diagram →

  1. CapturePanels tilt toward the sun, and piezo vibration shakes off dust before it builds up.
  2. ConvertThe MPPT converter keeps adjusting voltage so the array always runs at its peak power point.
  3. StoreSpare energy charges the battery for night-time and stormy days.
  4. DistributeThe power manager feeds each subsystem in order of priority.
  5. Survive & reportWhen energy runs low, ARES drops through survival tiers. All the while, it streams live telemetry to mission control.
