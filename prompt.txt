SECTION 1: ABOUT THE PROJECT

15. Is there a presentation, poster, or report that the website should match in style?

32. Do you want a demo video, a live demo, or a working prototype embedded?

35. Do you want an FAQ section?
36. Do you want a feedback or contact form? Where should messages go (email)?
37. Any links to include (GitHub, YouTube, Google Drive, social media)?

44. How should the site feel: modern, minimal, techy, futuristic, professional, playful, or academic?

56. Do you want social share buttons?
