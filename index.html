01 / The problem

On Mars, power decides how long a mission lasts.

Solar rovers rarely break down. More often they slowly run out of energy. ARES targets the power problems that actual missions ran into.

CASE 01 · DUST

Dust steals sunlight

Fine dust settles on solar panels and output drops sol after sol. Spirit and Opportunity had to wait for lucky gusts of wind to clean them.

CASE 02 · STORMS

Storms switch off the sun

In 2018 a dust storm that covered the whole planet blocked out the sun. Opportunity ran out of power and never woke up again.

CASE 03 · ANGLE

Bad angles waste power

Spirit got stuck in soft sand in 2009 and could not tilt its panels toward the winter sun. It went silent in 2010.

03 / Why we started

Why we started

Dhaka University has never had a rover team or entered a rover challenge. ARES is how we change that.
Team Solaris Tech

Every rover needs power before it can do anything else. So we started there, with the energy system.

ARES is our first step toward a full university rover team, built to compete in national and international rover challenges.
